The Logo Design Process: From Brief to Final Artwork

Published August 17, 2026

Follow a practical logo workflow from client discovery and sketches through vector artwork, revisions and final exports.

Begin with a brief

Understand the audience, personality, competitors and applications before sketching. A logo should solve a communication problem, not just look attractive.

Sketch before polishing

Generate several concepts quickly. Explore symbols, wordmarks and combinations before spending time perfecting one direction.

Test at multiple sizes

A strong logo remains recognizable on a social profile, business card, website header and large sign.

Practical takeaway

Before finishing a design, step back and ask whether the viewer can understand the main message quickly. A technically correct file is only successful when it communicates clearly.
