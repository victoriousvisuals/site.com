5 Branding Mistakes Small Businesses Make

Published August 17, 2026

Your brand is more than a logo. It is the visual and verbal impression customers get whenever they interact with your business. Consistency can make a small business look professional, memorable and trustworthy.

1. Using too many colours

Choose a small, repeatable colour palette and use it across your logo, social media graphics, website and printed materials.

2. Changing your logo everywhere

Keep approved logo versions and use them consistently so customers can recognize your business quickly.

3. Ignoring typography

Choose readable fonts that work well together and keep a consistent hierarchy for headings and body text.

4. Designing without a clear audience

Before creating a design, decide who should see it and what action you want that person to take.

5. Forgetting consistency

Your colours, fonts, layouts and messaging should feel like they belong to the same business across every platform.

Final thought

Good branding does not have to be complicated. Consistency, clarity and understanding your customers can make a big difference.
