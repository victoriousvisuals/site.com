Vector vs Raster Images Explained

Published August 17, 2026

Discover why logos are usually built as vectors while photographs are normally raster images, and why that matters for printing.

Contrast

Contrast separates important elements and improves readability. Contrast can come from size, weight, colour, shape or space.

Alignment

Alignment creates invisible relationships between elements. Use a consistent edge or grid instead of placing objects randomly.

Proximity and spacing

Related information should sit together while unrelated information should have enough space between it. Good spacing makes a design easier to scan.

Practical takeaway

Before finishing a design, step back and ask whether the viewer can understand the main message quickly. A technically correct file is only successful when it communicates clearly.
